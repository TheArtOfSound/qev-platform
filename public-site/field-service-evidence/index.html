QEV · Field service

Field-service job evidence

QEV captures selected job events into a sealed evidence package you control — not a claim that every system in the company is secured.

Typical flow

  1. Job created
  2. Estimate prepared / approved
  3. Before evidence
  4. Work started / completed
  5. After evidence
  6. Invoice / payment (optional)
  7. Package sealed + multi-verdict verify

What verification establishes

  • Package integrity digests match
  • Organization signature valid (when present)
  • Artifact fingerprints well-formed
  • Required evidence present or listed as gaps

What it does not establish

  • Workmanship quality
  • Legal liability outcomes
  • Content truth of source systems
